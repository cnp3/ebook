Conventions in the ebook
========================


The ebook assumes the following conventions.

1. We use American English spelling
2. The ebook is composed of parts, chapters, sections, subsections and sub-subsections using the following headings:


   ############
   Part heading
   ############

   ***************
   Chapter heading
   ***************


   Section heading
   ===============


   Sub-section heading
   -------------------

   Sub-subsection headting
   +++++++++++++++++++++++


3. Figures are identified using a label and referenced using :numref:
